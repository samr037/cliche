Install Cliché
==============

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

1. Drag Cliché onto the Applications folder.

2. Open Cliché from Applications. macOS refuses the first time because the app
   is not notarized by Apple:
   - open System Settings › Privacy & Security;
   - scroll to the message about Cliché and click "Open Anyway";
   - confirm with your password.

3. Allow "Screen Recording" when asked (System Settings › Privacy & Security ›
   Screen & System Audio Recording › enable Cliché). Scrolling capture also
   needs "Accessibility".

4. Cliché lives in the menu bar. Default shortcuts:
     ⇧⌘1  fullscreen      ⇧⌘2  area         ⇧⌘6  window
     ⇧⌘7  scrolling       ⇧⌘8  text (OCR)   ⇧⌘9  repeat area
   Change them in Settings › Shortcuts.

Everything stays on your Mac: no account, no upload, no telemetry.
